How to read it. The coast number is your target discounted back to today at the real return — hit it and you could stop contributing entirely. The green line is where your current monthly savings actually take you; the brass line is the minimum you'd need at each age to coast the rest of the way. Where they cross, saving becomes optional. Real return means after inflation, so every figure is in today's purchasing power.
